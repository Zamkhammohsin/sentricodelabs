The Client & Industry Profile

The client is a premier, global freight broker based in Rotterdam, handling high-volume, multi-modal logistics across massive international marine hubs and transport networks. Operating at the epicenter of European trade, they coordinate intricate schedules spanning shipping containers, dry van trucking fleets, and urgent air freight operations.

The Business Challenge

As shipping volumes scaled rapidly, the client's legacy tracking software began failing under pressure. Severe database locks occurred routinely during peak harbor scheduling windows. This led to order synchronization lag exceeding 12 minutes, forcing operators to execute manual check-ins via phone and email.

"Without real-time data sync, we were operating in a blind spot. Even a minor schedule drift at international harbors threatened key delivery windows across our entire continental logistics pipeline."

This friction severely damaged supply chain trust, compromised driver schedules, and introduced costly demurrage fees. The client urgently needed a robust, low-latency tracking system capable of real-time multi-modal logistics coordination.

The Architectural Solution

Sentricodelabs engineered a state-of-the-art serverless database architecture and API layer designed to process real-time events concurrently without execution bottlenecks.

1. Distributed Edge API: We deployed high-performance globally distributed API endpoints capable of processing asynchronous updates under 100 milliseconds, minimizing origin server overhead.

2. Real-time GPS Tracking: Integrated high-frequency telematics to ingestion pipelines, providing smooth, constant coordinate updates across all fleet operations.

3. Visual Dashboard Interface: Formulated an elegant, real-time reactive management panel displaying instant harbor, rail, and road transfer updates.

By decommissioning the legacy database systems and implementing a cutting-edge serverless tracking infrastructure, Sentricodelabs completely transformed the operational efficiency of the client's global shipping network:

  • Near-Instantaneous Sync: Order sync latency was slashed from a crippling 12-minute delay down to an average of less than 1.8 seconds.

  • Operational Cost Reductions: Eliminated manual check-ins completely, enabling coordinators to oversee 40% more concurrent shipments.
